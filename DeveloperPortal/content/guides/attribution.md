# Credit

Coasterpedia's editors write the data, and it's licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). You can use
it in any project, commercial or not, as long as you credit Coasterpedia and
share what you make from the data under the same licence.

## Where to put the credit

Wherever your project shows the data, say where it came from. This line is
enough:

```html
Ride data from <a href="https://coasterpedia.net">Coasterpedia</a>, CC BY-SA 4.0
```

Put it where people see the data, on the screen or page that shows it. If
the data runs all through an app, the app's about screen will do. For a bot,
a command that shows the credit will do. If you changed the data, say so.

Every response includes the licence in `meta.licence`, so your project can
show it without you writing it in by hand.

## Share-alike

Share-alike applies to the data. If you publish a dataset that includes ours,
or one you made from it, you must publish that dataset under CC BY-SA 4.0
too. Your own code, design and other content around it can use any licence
you like. This is only a summary. If it and the
[licence itself](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode)
differ, the licence is what counts.

## The showcase

Before we list a project in [the showcase](/showcase), we check that it
works and that it shows the credit.

The [API terms](/terms/) cover how you use the service, such as your key
and your limits. They're separate from the data's licence, and they can't take away
anything the licence lets you do with the data.
